一句话

这篇论文聚焦“Agentic 多模态大模型合并”这一具体任务，目标是在把面向不同工具或环境的 agentic MLLM 合并为单一通用模型时，修复合并后在弱任务上的性能退化与长程决策行为遗忘问题。作者提出一个免训练的粗到细修复框架 AgentPatch，据摘要称可在六个 agentic/多模态基准上改善多种 merged backbone 的弱任务恢复与能力平衡。

贡献

从摘要看，核心贡献有三点：一是把“Agentic MLLM Merging”明确表述为一个独立问题，并指出两类关键失败模式——不对称能力保留导致的 weak-task degradation，以及 behavior-critical forgetting 导致的长时执行偏航；二是提出训练自由的 AgentPatch，包括 Weak-Task Unique Residual Recovery 和 Agent-Guided Behavior-Critical Patch 两个阶段，用于从粗到细恢复任务特异信号与关键行为；三是强调输出是单一静态 checkpoint，而不是路由、集成或多模型切换，这对部署和推理成本有直接意义。

1问题

论文想解决的具体问题是：当多个面向不同工具、环境或任务生态的 agentic 多模态大模型被合并为单一模型时，为什么某些能力会被不均匀保留，尤其出现弱任务性能明显下降，以及为什么长程交互中少数决定性动作的遗忘会导致整体执行失败；进一步地，能否在不重新训练的前提下，对已有 merged model 进行系统性修复。

2方法

从摘要推测，方法流程可能分为三步。先获得或挑选一个“稳定”的 merged backbone，作为统一修复对象；再进行 Weak-Task Unique Residual Recovery，从单任务专家或任务差分中恢复那些在 merge 中被稀释的弱任务特异残差信号；最后做 Agent-Guided Behavior-Critical Patch，利用 agent 轨迹、关键动作分析或专家指导，针对会决定长程成功/失败的行为位置注入补丁，并通过显式能力保护约束减少对其他任务的副作用。实验上应是在六个 agentic/多模态 benchmark 上，对多种 merged backbones 比较 patch 前后表现，并观察弱任务恢复、总体均衡性和长程执行稳定性。

3价值

最值得关注的亮点有四个。第一，问题定义具体，直接针对 agentic MLLM 合并而非普通 LLM/MLLM merge，抓住了工具使用和环境交互带来的长程行为脆弱性。第二，提出了两个很有辨识度的失效概念：weak-task degradation 与 behavior-critical forgetting，这有助于把 merge 失败从“平均分下降”细化到任务级和行为级分析。第三，方法主打 training-free 且输出单一静态 checkpoint，如果属实，部署复杂度和推理成本会明显优于路由或集成式方案。第四，摘要声称适用于 diverse merged backbones，意味着它可能是一个较通用的后处理修复框架，而不是绑定某个专有模型。

American bison ( Bison bison ) were nearly hunted to extinction by the 20th century. Abstract Understanding how adverse life events trigger stress-related behavioural changes remains an unresolved challenge. The amygdala is integral to emotion and stress responses 1 and comprises astrocytes, neurons and other cells. Here we show that amygdala astrocytes contribute to stress-related behaviours through signalling mechanisms related to their primary cilia 2 . Amygdala astrocytes are altered during stress at the protein and gene expression level, display reduced expression of molecules related to primary cilia 3,4 and have morphologically short primary cilia 5,6 . G protein-coupled receptors (GPCRs) are central to astrocyte 7 and primary cilia 2,8,9 function. Therefore, we speculated that GPCR signalling activation might be beneficial in stress-related behavioural disorders. We identified amygdala astrocyte GPCRs as regulators of responses following stress. Chemogenetics and targeting of native sphingosine-1-phosphate receptor 1 (S1PR1) GPCRs led to the restoration of astrocyte primary cilia length, corrected molecular alterations and improved stress-related behaviours. Cilium-related genes were abundantly expressed in human amygdala astrocytes, with many displaying disrupted expression in stress-related brain disorders. S1PR1 was also highly expressed in amygdala astrocytes from human tissue. Selective genetic disruption of amygdala astrocyte primary cilia in mice altered some stress-related behaviours and gene expression of astrocytes and parenchymal cells. These data confirm that astrocytic cilia have important roles in this brain nucleus. In summary, amygdala astrocytes and their primary cilia are disrupted during stress, and their restoration is accompanied by stress-related molecular and behavioural improvements. Astrocyte primary cilia-related mechanisms may therefore provide new treatment strategies for stress-related and other brain disorders.
