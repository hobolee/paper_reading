一句话

这篇论文提出 EnvOS，用于审计会在执行过程中发生变化的 computer-use agents 环境，重点针对“陈旧信息”现象：智能体先看到某个值，随后该值在任务中途被更新，再考察其是否能在诊所预约和保险理赔审批等任务中正确应对。摘要显示作者不仅构造了这种动态任务，还在智能体运行前后分别做环境变更触发、任务可解性、评分器区分能力与基于归档证据的复核，以减少把环境或评分错误误判为智能体失败。

原始摘要

Human activities are eroding biodiversity, especially in freshwater systems. Despite major advances in quantifying species vulnerability and threats, we still lack a crucial understanding of where threats are affecting freshwater species globally. Here, we produced a species-centered spatial assessment of threats, mapping the impact of 45 threats across 21,490 freshwater species of amphibians, fish, mammals, odonates, and reptiles. We found that species in highly developed and water-managed regions are strongly affected by multiple overlapping threats. Although threats vary across taxa and regions, increasing threat probabilities elevate extinction risk and population declines, with freshwater species experiencing greater threat intensity and distinct spatial patterns than terrestrial species. Our findings establish a spatially explicit baseline for targeting the most urgently needed freshwater conservation and management interventions.

原始摘要

Abstract Obesity, which is often associated with prolonged exposure to an obesogenic diet, substantially alters immunity 1–7 , but whether brief dietary changes shape acute inflammatory responses and long-term tissue memory remains unclear. Here we show that transient high-fat diet (HFD) exposure at the onset of acute type 17 skin inflammation exacerbates disease in lean mice to levels comparable to those associated with chronic obesity. This effect is restricted to a discrete 4-day peri-initiation window; HFD outside this window has no impact, whereas switching obese mice to a low-fat diet (LFD) during a similar window fully blunts disease amplification. Mechanistically, HFD rapidly recruits neutrophils into the skin, and neutrophils in challenged skin express high levels of Nlrp3 (ref. 8 ) and Il1b . Neutrophil depletion, genetic Nlrp3 deficiency and pharmacological NLRP3 inhibition each blunt the HFD-induced inflammatory amplification. These findings nominate a neutrophil–NLRP3–IL-1β axis that licences pathogenic IL-1R1 + CD4 + T helper type 17 (T H 17) cells 9,10 . After recovery, a chromatin-poised tissue-resident memory T cell population persists. Consequently, mice exposed to HFD at disease onset develop exaggerated inflammation upon recurrence, despite recovery and maintenance on LFD. Transient HFD similarly amplifies lung T H 17 inflammation through neutrophil- and IL-1R1-dependent mechanisms. Together, these findings show that dietary state during a narrow temporal window can durably reprogram disease trajectory, suggesting that short-lived physiological states may become embedded in local immune memory 11 when they coincide with tissue inflammation.
