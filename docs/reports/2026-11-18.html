1. When Tool Names Are Not Identities: Measuring Provider Misbinding in MCP-Based AI Agents

⭐ 👍 👎
Zeynab Anbiaee、Sajjad Dadkhah、Ali A. Ghorbani · PDF
large language model 1 llm 1 language model 1 agent 1 agents 1 ai agent 1 ai agents 1 ai 1 environment 1
一句话

该论文聚焦 MCP（Model Context Protocol）生态中 LLM 智能体的工具调用安全，具体研究“工具名/元数据未与提供者身份可靠绑定”时，客户端可能把原本应发往合法提供者的请求错误解析到攻击者控制工具上的“provider misbinding”问题。作者在受控 MCP 环境中、面向五种基于 LLM 的客户端，考察了工具名称冲突、描述对齐、参数模式兼容等因素如何诱导错误工具选择。

贡献

从摘要看，论文的核心贡献是把 MCP 多服务器工具组合场景中的错误提供者解析形式化为“攻击者可控的工具选择优化问题”，并在不假设主机、模型、合法服务器或网络被攻破的前提下，系统测量仅靠伪造工具元数据即可造成的误绑定风险。另一个重要贡献是给出较具体的失效条件线索：精确名称碰撞、任务对齐描述与兼容 schema 叠加时风险最强，而带有权威感的描述加兼容 schema 即使没有完全同名，也可能导致错误执行或解析失败。

原始摘要

Abstract Transcriptional addiction to the androgen receptor (AR) underlies metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer 1 , in which AR maintains oncogenic programs through domain-specific interactions with the acetyltransferases p300 and CBP (collectively p300/CBP) 2 . Here we describe a novel therapeutic modality, domain-alteration chimeras (DALTACs), that rewire endogenous protein complexes by enforcing non-native domain–domain interactions. Our first-in-class AR–p300/CBP DALTAC-1 induces synthetic proximity between the ligand-binding domain of AR and the bromodomain of p300/CBP, misconfiguring their native interface and locking the complex in a non-productive state. DALTAC-1 triggers a ‘super-inhibitory’ effect, suppressing AR-driven transcription and proliferation more potently than combined AR and p300/CBP inhibition. Mechanistically, DALTAC-1 reprograms p300/CBP substrate engagement, abolishing the histone mark H2B N-terminal acetylation and inducing neomorphic acetylation of AR and coactivators, ultimately collapsing the AR neo-enhanceosome. Chromatin profiling revealed redistribution of AR and p300/CBP towards palindromic androgen response elements, accompanied by disruption of cofactor and transcriptional machinery recruitment at oncogenic AR and ERG neo-enhancers. DALTAC-1 exhibits exquisite lineage selectivity, displaying potent activity in AR–p300/CBP dual-dependent cells and sparing AR-negative cells. In multiple in vivo models, DALTAC-1 induces deep and durable tumour regressions. Together, these findings establish DALTACs as a broadly applicable strategy to rewire disease-defining complexes through domain-topology alteration, expanding induced-proximity therapies, and the precise lineage selectivity of DALTAC-1 supports its translational potential in AR-driven prostate cancer.
