一句话

这篇论文聚焦带外部工具的长时程LLM智能体，在需要持续环境交互的复杂任务中，针对“早期执行错误会沿决策链累积并破坏后续状态”的问题，提出了一个名为ReflectPO的强化学习框架，用于自主错误恢复。根据摘要，它主要在两个长时程工具使用基准上评估，通过反思引导采样和树结构信用分配来优化策略。

贡献

从题名和摘要看，核心贡献应包括：1）提出Reflection-Guided Policy Optimization（ReflectPO），面向长时程工具使用型LLM agent的错误恢复；2）设计Reflection-Guided Sampling，利用交互轨迹中的分支差异和重复访问现象构造恢复提示；3）设计Tree-Structured Credit Assignment，将采样轨迹组织为树，并基于父子奖励差与恢复增益进行动作级信用分配；4）在两个长时程tool-use benchmark上与多种基线比较，报告多数领域优于现有方法。

原始摘要

Abstract WEE1 phosphorylates and inhibits the activity of cyclin-dependent kinases to prevent mitotic entry and establish a G2 DNA damage checkpoint 1–3 . Consequently, WEE1 inhibitors have been developed to be synthetically lethal in TP53- mutated tumours that become reliant on this WEE1-mediated checkpoint, and such inhibitors are undergoing clinical trials. However, WEE1 inhibitors also have efficacy in TP53- wild-type tumours, and many trials have been terminated owing to high levels of toxic side effects, suggesting that WEE1 has unknown functions. Here we show that WEE1 promotes cell cycle re-entry from quiescence (G0) by opposing the activity of the E3 ubiquitin ligase, anaphase-promoting complex–cyclosome bound to CDH1 (APC/C CDH1 ). WEE1 phosphorylates CDH1 ( FZR1 ) at key residues that mediate the interaction between CDH1 and APC/C. Cells with loss of function of WEE1 during G0/G1 have delayed S phase entry, impaired G1/S transition, abnormal S phase accumulation of cyclin-dependent kinase inhibitor p21 Cip1/Kip1 and a p21-dependent G2 arrest. Reduced expression of APC/C CDH1 or p21 renders cells more sensitive to acute WEE1 inhibition, and both pathways are downregulated in cells with acquired WEE1 inhibitor resistance. These results reveal a mechanism of cell cycle control that has implications for how WEE1 inhibitors should be used in the clinic.
